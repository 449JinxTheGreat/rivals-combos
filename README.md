# rivals-combos

Combo data for **Combo Coach**, a combo trainer app for Marvel Rivals.

*Fan-made. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Marvel or NetEase. Marvel Rivals is a trademark of its owners.*

The app downloads its combo data from here to stay up to date. It is a guide only: no cheats, nothing touches the game.

- `combos.json` holds every hero's combos, abilities, playstyle and matchups.
- `version` goes up by 1 each time the combos change, and that's how the app knows to update.
